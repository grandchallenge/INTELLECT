## Recitals

The compact text in `CONSTITUTION.md` is the constitutional law of the Grand
Intellect. A larger account of the institution is useful for interpretation
and judgment but must not become a second, competing constitution.

Grand Challenge Labs also requires a GitHub operating standard. That standard
must govern use of the platform without displacing the Constitution,
INTELLECT's constitutional policy, AETHER's production semantic authority,
programme authority, or MATHCERT's bounded mathematical adjudication.

## Article 1: Preservation of constitutional law

1. `CONSTITUTION.md` remains the supreme constitutional law of the Grand
   Intellect.
2. This amendment supplements that Constitution. It does not replace, restate,
   or silently edit it.
3. The Constitution prevails over commentary, standards, profiles, programme
   adoption records, repository settings, automation, and platform state.

## Article 2: Commentary and operating doctrine

1. `docs/CONSTITUTIONAL_COMMENTARY_AND_OPERATING_DOCTRINE.md` is designated the
   official interpretive commentary and operating doctrine.
2. The commentary explains purpose, offices, modes, failure patterns, and
   proportional implementation. It is not independently binding.
3. Commentary may guide judgment where the Constitution permits discretion.
   It may not add an office power, remove a gate obligation, authorize an
   irreversible act, or resolve a conflict against the Constitution.
4. A rule stated only in commentary becomes binding only through a lawful
   amendment or a subordinate standard adopted within delegated authority.

## Article 3: Authority chain

The following authority chain governs:

1. the accountable Human Steward exercises the reserved powers in Article X;
2. the Constitution governs powers, obligations, transitions, records, and
   limits;
3. INTELLECT implements constitutional policy, office obligations,
   work-package commands, artifact contracts, projections, and gate reports;
4. AETHER remains the production authority for append order, semantic cuts,
   replay, policy visibility, provenance-bearing facts, recursive derivation,
   and proof traces;
5. `gcl-standards` registers and publishes cross-programme operating standards
   admitted through constitutional process;
6. programme repositories adopt exact standard versions and commits only
   within their delegated domain authority;
7. repositories and GitHub capabilities author, review, integrate, execute,
   index, and publish artifacts and evidence.

No lower layer may enlarge its own authority by configuration, adoption,
automation, or publication.

## Article 4: Domain authority

1. `MATH-PROGRAMME` retains mathematics-specific policy, integration,
   publication, and archival authority, subject to the Constitution.
2. `MATHCERT` alone renders bounded mathematical certification dispositions
   through an accepted certification route.
3. A Council or Referee disposition controls institutional admission or
   closure under declared criteria. It does not by itself certify a
   mathematical claim.
4. A cross-programme standard may define operational obligations. It may not
   alter a programme's domain authority without constitutional authorization.

## Article 5: GitHub boundary

1. GitHub is an operational and evidentiary substrate, not a constitutional or
   production semantic authority.
2. Issues may own next actions; pull requests may integrate authoritative
   repository artifacts; Actions may produce evidence; Projects may index
   work; Discussions may host nonbinding deliberation; environments may gate
   publication or administration; releases may publish admitted artifacts.
3. No issue state, review state, check result, Project field, Discussion
   consensus, environment approval, release, package, Pages deployment, app,
   token, ruleset, or repository setting may by itself:
   - amend the Constitution;
   - establish an AETHER production fact;
   - certify mathematics;
   - upgrade a claim beyond its authoritative domain record; or
   - substitute for a required office finding.
4. GitHub evidence admitted into production coordination must retain exact
   artifact identity, commit identity, provenance, and the applicable AETHER
   reference.

## Article 6: Reconciliation of operating concepts

1. The constitutional phases remain those listed in Article IV.
2. “Renewal” in the commentary means the lawful reopening of a Complete work
   package to Generation. It is not a tenth phase.
3. Artifact profiles and operating modes may vary formality and the physical
   manifestation of records in proportion to risk, novelty, and
   irreversibility. They may not waive the substantive conditions or office
   approvals in Article V.
4. Repository and claim-status vocabularies are projections of their
   authoritative records. They do not create a second lifecycle.
5. The expanded “Compact” is interpretive only. The Compact in
   `CONSTITUTION.md` is the authoritative compact.

## Article 7: Adoption of GCL-GHOS

1. `GCL-GHOS-00` may be admitted as the umbrella's subordinate GitHub operating
   standard only by exact version and 40-character commit after this amendment
   is effective.
2. Constitutional review may bind an exact candidate standard commit as a
   subject of the amendment packet. Candidate review does not accept the ADR,
   admit the standard, or create programme adoption.
3. After this amendment becomes effective, the Council may accept ADR-0001 and
   admit the reviewed GCL-GHOS revision through a protected `gcl-standards`
   integration record.
4. Each programme may then adopt the admitted standard by recording its exact
   version, commit, decision, review receipt, and unresolved deviations.
5. `gcl-standards` is the versioned registry and publication repository for an
   admitted standard. Custody of the text is not constitutional ownership.
6. A conflict is resolved in this order: Constitution, effective amendments,
   INTELLECT constitutional schedules, accepted cross-programme standards,
   programme adoption records, repository profiles, then GitHub projections.

## Article 8: Executable activation gate

1. `governance/constitutional_authority_schedule.json` is the machine-readable
   activation record for this amendment.
2. The schedule must remain `proposed` until it records:
   - substantive Human Steward approval;
   - a non-author agent Adversary review;
   - a different non-author agent Referee review of the exact revision;
   - a complete constitutional review receipt;
   - the exact reviewed INTELLECT commit;
   - the exact reviewed candidate `gcl-standards` commit; and
   - an effective timestamp.
3. Amendment activation does not require prior acceptance of ADR-0001,
   admission of GCL-GHOS, or MATH-PROGRAMME adoption. Those are subordinate
   post-activation decisions governed by Article 7.
4. While `GI-STEWARD-0001` is effective, the Council Clerk may assemble one
   immutable, digest-addressed packet for all coordinated repositories, admit
   separate structured agent Adversary and Referee findings, and publish a
   Human Steward attestation. The named Human Steward signs only by an
   authenticated action on the current packet after substantive inspection.
5. Automation shall reject stale packets, proposal authors acting as
   independent reviewers, reuse of one agent identity, invocation, or session
   as both Adversary and Referee, incomplete findings, incomplete checks,
   circular activation dependencies, and non-steward authorization.
6. Activation validation shall load the exact receipt named by the schedule and
   cross-bind its campaign identity, packet digest, proposal authors, reviewed
   subject heads, agent identities and sessions, and attestation references to
   the activation record. A syntactically valid but unrelated receipt does not
   satisfy this gate.
7. Automation may transcribe accepted agent findings and the authenticated
   Human Steward attestation into a review receipt and propose an activation
   record. It may not impersonate the Steward, approve or merge its own work,
   activate the amendment, accept ADR-0001, admit GCL-GHOS, or adopt a standard
   for a programme.
8. The executable validator and negative tests must reject authority inversion,
   incomplete activation, receipt substitution, subject or reviewer drift,
   premature standard admission, premature programme adoption, and reuse of
   stale findings.
9. Merge, CI success, release, publication, assignment, or presence does not
   satisfy an unrecorded approval.

## Article 9: Effect

The activation record satisfied Article 8 and recorded Human Steward
promulgation, making this amendment effective as Constitution version 1.1.0.
`GI-STEWARD-0001` changed who could staff the review offices during bootstrap;
it did not make this amendment self-ratifying. At activation, ADR-0001
acceptance, GCL-GHOS admission, and programme adoption remained later
subordinate decisions. Before activation was recorded, this amendment was a
reviewable proposal and created no binding authority.
