# Specification - bounded executive-polity evaluation

Status: proposed evaluation contract; Charter gate remains subject to fresh review. This design does not start the pilot, authorize any corporate act, contact a participant, or allocate paid resources.

## Institutional outcome

For each eligible matter, record the existing programme commitment or institutional need it supports, the obstacle being addressed, its observable disposition and any residual obstacle. Corporate-private identities stay in private GCT custody; public evidence uses permitted pseudonymous references. This field explains the use of executive capacity without changing the five issued matter classes, selecting cases for favourable research outcomes or giving GCT authority over scientific acceptance.

Report observed Founder, facilitator and reviewer effort separately and their total where all components are measured. Where a component is missing, total effort is unknown. Report obstacle disposition separately from interruption and time comparisons; neither constitutes causal research acceleration or scientific success.

## Fixed envelope

At most ten real non-reserved matters: at most two in each of the five charter classes. Include the first two eligible matters per class in intake timestamp order after a frozen start marker; preserve exclusions and reasons. End after fourteen calendar days, ten matters, or eight person-hours of facilitator/reviewer effort, whichever occurs first. A matter gets at most two bounded recovery attempts and sixty minutes of active evaluation effort. No paid service, new credential or external outreach is introduced. These are maximum design limits, not resource authorizations. If an existing authorization does not cover a matter, route it out without execution.

Before observation, freeze class definitions, eligibility/exclusion rules, the intake order, start marker, manifest format and applicable existing authority references in private GCT custody. Prospective matter identities cannot be frozen before they arrive: append each eligible matter and exclusion in intake order before its evaluation, without replacing entries after outcomes. Freeze the baseline pool and comparator selection rule before the start marker; select and record the most recent eligible same-class comparator before examining that matter's prospective outcome. Do not fill unavailable classes with invented live matters or change sampling after outcomes. Fewer than five represented classes means incomplete coverage, not failure to be hidden. The public return contains only sanitized counts and evidence references permitted by that custody boundary.

## Baseline and comparison

For each prospective matter, select the most recent completed ordinary-workflow matter in the same class before the start marker, using the same non-reserved eligibility and available measurement fields. Freeze this matching rule and the baseline identities before examining prospective outcomes. If no eligible comparator exists, mark that pair unmatched. Do not replay a historical matter as a new corporate act. The baseline is the recorded ordinary workflow, not a deliberately disadvantaged reconstruction.

Compare matched class-level interruption counts, measured Founder active time, closure time, retry/recovery counts, unresolved review debt and stale-state events. Preserve paired raw values and denominators. Closure time includes waiting; active time requires actual recorded start/stop observations. Never substitute message interval for active work. Missing duration remains missing, with reason; do not impute zero or use a guessed average. Report count comparisons even when time comparison is unavailable.

This is an observational feasibility pilot with time-order, case-complexity and selection confounding. Matching by class does not establish causal savings. No population, productivity or corporate-benefit claim follows from ten cases. Partial timing cannot establish a timed improvement.

## Route and review record

Each sampled matter records exact source/artifact revisions, admitted authority basis, classification, chosen route, action or rejection, recovery, applicable reviewer provenance, Founder escalations, timestamps actually observed, terminal disposition and residual debt. Reserved or ambiguous matters stop at escalation or rejection; the evaluation must not perform the reserved act to complete a row. At least one consequential matter needs a genuinely non-sole reviewer under the Charter; if none is available, that acceptance criterion remains unmet.

## Predeclared disposition rubric

- HOLD immediately on an unauthorized reserved action, custody breach, unverifiable action authority, or loss of necessary provenance. Preserve and route incident evidence; no retry may erase it.
- NARROW a class if it lacks a reconstructible route, requires unsupported authority, exhausts its recovery cap, or has insufficient baseline/measurement to answer its decision question.
- ACTIVE if safe and reconstructible but comparative benefit is inconclusive, coverage incomplete, or review obligations remain.
- Recommend ACCELERATE only for a class with two complete eligible pairs, no boundary violation, all applicable review obligations discharged, interruption count no higher in either pair, and fewer interruptions in at least one. Where time is measured in both pairs it must not contradict the recommendation; missing timing prohibits a time-savings claim. Include total observed effort and the supported institutional need in the recommendation. If measured total effort increases, record any compensating declared benefit and recommend ACTIVE or NARROW until GCT separately judges that trade-off; interruption reduction alone cannot justify ACCELERATE. This is a bounded follow-on recommendation, not autonomous resource escalation.
- RETIRE the proposed workflow for a class if both complete pairs add interruption burden with no compensating declared governance benefit, or the process cannot remain within the authority/custody envelope. Preserve why and the conditions for reconsideration.

Overall report shows class outcomes rather than concealing disagreement in an average. No inferential statistical claim is attempted. Reversal conditions are new contradictory evidence, changed governing authority, changed workflow version, or a newly observed boundary failure. Such a change requires a new frozen design before further observations.

## Return and stopping

Return all sampled and excluded cases, missingness, failures, matched/unmatched counts, class recommendations and exact reviewer provenance. Human activity is observed only where separately available and appropriate; this is not employee-performance evaluation. Stop on any cap or authority/custody uncertainty. Do not recruit a participant or begin execution merely because this specification is committed.
