## Authority

The committee may research, draft policy and schemas, build isolated or local
validators, create adversarial tests, measure costs and bottlenecks, and run
non-authoritative shadow evaluations.

The committee may not:

- approve or merge its own work;
- alter current human-review requirements;
- create a live proxy delegation or credential;
- impersonate or manufacture a human act;
- change protected settings, identities, permissions, credentials, workflows,
  authority schedules, office powers, or current effective policy;
- activate, ratify, admit, adopt, promote, waive, certify, or make a public or
  major resource commitment; or
- exercise AETHER or MATHCERT authority.

GI-STEWARD-0002 and all other current rules remain effective.

## Ordered work programme

1. **Baseline and inventory.** Measure approval volume, human actions, wait
   time, operator minutes, exceptions, incidents, maintenance cost,
   repositories, current bots, credentials, approval rules, and downstream
   consumers.
2. **Vocabulary and authority model.** Define qualifying human act, delegation
   principal, machine proxy, attributed proxy act, office disposition, policy
   eligibility, execution permission, routine task, control-plane effect,
   integration, readback, revocation, compensation, and non-amplification.
3. **Contracts and schemas.** Specify TaskClass, ControlPlaneCapability,
   Delegation, ProxyIdentity, CredentialLease, TaskInstance, OfficeDecision,
   Hold, Revocation, Exception, Incident, ProxyAct, IntegrationReceipt,
   ReadbackReceipt, Compensation, Supersession, and TerminalStatus.
4. **Classifier and denied capabilities.** Produce a closed initial allowlist,
   semantic dependency-closure classifier, and explicit control-plane registry.
5. **Identity, credentials, and independence.** Define authenticated principals,
   proxy workloads, reviewer sessions, incompatible roles, task-bound leases,
   atomic revocation, replay consumption, holds, and execution-time checks.
6. **Custody and migration.** Define INTELLECT governance custody, the AETHER
   production-evidence boundary, lineage, retention, recovery, existing-system
   migration, grandfathering, suspension, and decommissioning.
7. **Threat model and tests.** Exercise impersonation, authority laundering,
   self or sybil review, confused deputy, replay, revocation races, task
   composition, transitive workflow effects, excess credentials, spoofed
   checks, indirect control-plane mutation, evidence gaps, and partial failure.
8. **Bounded shadow pilot.** Evaluate at most three closed task classes across
   at most two repositories. It may not replace required human review or cause
   protected integration carrying institutional effect.
9. **Article XI package.** Produce the ADR, threat analysis, compatibility and
   migration plan, updated gates and tests, and proposed explicit effective
   version.
10. **Terminal packet.** Bind all work products and residual uncertainty into
    one exact packet for renewed full-Council review.

## Shadow acceptance thresholds

- zero unauthorized proxy acts;
- zero false-routine classifications;
- complete detection of seeded control-plane and attack cases;
- zero human-attribution errors;
- zero replay, revocation, hold, or scope bypasses;
- deterministic reconstruction from canonical records;
- at least 70 percent reduction in median human handling time for each pilot
  class; and
- positive human-time savings after maintenance and incident effort.

Failure of a safety threshold rejects the affected task class. Failure to
produce the complete packet by the deadline closes the committee with current
rules unchanged.

## Terminal route

The committee terminates with exactly one of:

- `complete_packet_returned_for_renewed_full_council_review`;
- `incomplete_at_deadline_current_rules_preserved`; or
- `committee_abandoned_with_reasons_and_current_rules_preserved`.
