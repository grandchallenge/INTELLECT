# Compatibility and migration plan

1. Admit the exact successor directive under `GI-STEWARD-0002` review rules.
2. Record one packet-bound Human Steward authorization covering activation and
   the listed deterministic downstream operations.
3. Select `GI-STEWARD-0003` in the protected authority schedule and verify
   protected-main readback.
4. Admit `GCL-AGENT-STAFFING-001` version `1.0.0` in `gcl-standards`.
5. Update organization defaults and all active repositories with exact adoption
   records, preserving their narrower authority exceptions.
6. Publish a 16-repository conformance matrix with zero unresolved rollout
   obligations.

In-flight exact packets may finish under their recorded predecessor rules or be
restarted. They may not be relabelled. Historical records remain immutable.
