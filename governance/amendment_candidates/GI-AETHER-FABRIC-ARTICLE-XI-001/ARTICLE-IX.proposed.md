## Article IX: Semantic authority

AETHER is the authoritative semantic coordination substrate within its admitted production domain.

AETHER owns append order, semantic cuts, replay, policy visibility, provenance-bearing facts, recursive derivation, proof traces, and semantic admission within that domain. Semantic namespace resolution, same-namespace operation ordering, started-operation lifecycle, atomicity, leader epochs, prefix validation, and stale-authority fencing remain AETHER responsibilities.

FABRIC may provide mechanical transport, rendezvous, placement, streaming, replication, backpressure, and availability services only within attributable, bounded, versioned mechanical authorization envelopes. It may narrow or reject an envelope but shall not mint or widen semantic, institutional, constitutional, or execution-controller authority. Delivery, receipts, liveness, locality, replication, resource leases, and availability do not constitute semantic admission or institutional permission.

INTELLECT owns constitutional policy, office obligations, work-package commands, artifact contracts, application projections, and gate reports. GHOS controller admission and protected execution remain separately governed; endpoint identity does not establish controller identity.

Mechanical observations may be submitted as provenance-bearing evidence. They shall not directly mutate semantic policy or institutional allocation. Unknown, delayed, or undelivered information shall not be treated as false or rejected semantic evidence. Operational scheduling must preserve AETHER's semantic start/completion and cancellation contract.

AETHER shall remain locally semantically correct without FABRIC. Moving any responsibility requires exact-version conformance and hostile evidence, compatibility and rollback, and every applicable domain authorization. This Article does not itself authorize extraction, deployment, a FABRIC runtime or repository, AETHER-POL or AETHER-Learn activation, public commitments, or a resource allocation.

An in-memory fabric may be used for tests and pedagogy. It shall not be represented as production semantic authority. This historical term does not identify or authorize a production FABRIC service.
