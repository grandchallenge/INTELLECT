**Submitted:** 2026-08-12

**Source committee:** `GI-PROXY-DELEGATION-COMMITTEE-001`

**Requested disposition:** `table_for_human_steward_disposition`

**Authority boundary:** Schedule amendment only; no proxy authority or human-review exception

## Motion

Replace the committee's thirty-day execution window with one exact duration of
`PT72H`. The accelerated window begins only when a ratification/opening record
binds its UTC `effective_at`, UTC `deadline`, this proposal, the source Council
disposition, the committee membership, the authority boundary, and the artifact
manifest. The deadline must equal `effective_at + PT72H`.

Until that record exists, the existing deadline remains controlling. No
retrospective start or inferred ratification is permitted.

## Three-day programme

1. **T+0 through T+24:** freeze the candidate scope, baseline, vocabulary,
   authority model, contracts, classifier, denied capabilities, identity and
   custody topology, role separation, and pilot protocol.
2. **T+24 through T+48:** implement isolated validators and hostile tests;
   complete compatibility, migration, recovery, revocation, replay, hold,
   evidence, failure, and compensation contracts. Any incomplete or failed
   safety predicate blocks the affected class from shadow evaluation.
3. **T+48 through T+60:** run non-authoritative historical or isolated shadow
   replay for at most one closed task class in one repository.
4. **T+60:** freeze substantive artifacts.
5. **T+60 through T+66:** assemble the Article XI candidate, evidence manifest,
   measurements, residual uncertainty, and rejected-class records.
6. **T+66 through T+70:** perform independent reproduction, digest/readback,
   and verification from a clean state.
7. **T+70 through T+72:** permit only Amanuensis lineage and Referee
   completeness assessment. No substantive patching is allowed.

## Mandatory gates

The acceleration narrows scope, not proof. All original committee safety gates
remain mandatory. Submission additionally requires:

- exact canonical identity for every artifact and result;
- complete required schemas and fail-closed validators;
- zero false-routine classifications and complete seeded-negative detection;
- zero impersonation, self-review, scope, credential, replay, revocation, hold,
  attribution, integration, or readback bypasses;
- deterministic reconstruction from canonical records;
- at least 70 percent median human-handling-time reduction and positive
  maintenance-adjusted savings for the candidate class;
- a complete Article XI candidate package;
- ten distinct committee-office terminal records bound to one packet digest;
  and
- a Referee completeness finding.

Any absent, ambiguous, flaky, stale, failed, or unreproducible requirement is a
failure. At T+72 the committee must record exactly one existing terminal outcome.
Time expiry never converts unknown or incomplete evidence into acceptance.

## Ratification and effectiveness

Full-quorum unanimous Council concurrence may table this schedule amendment for
a separate, authenticated Human Steward disposition. That disposition may start
the `PT72H` committee sprint but cannot approve the underlying proxy proposal.

A successful committee sprint may submit only an exact candidate packet for
renewed full-Council review. The underlying proxy proposal still requires a
separate unanimous Council disposition, substantive Human Steward approval,
exact-candidate validation, protected integration, an explicit effective
version, and independent readback. Current human-review requirements remain
effective throughout.
