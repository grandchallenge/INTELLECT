# GI-STEWARD-0001: Temporary Steward-supervised agent staffing

**Status:** Effective
**Issued:** 2026-07-29
**Amended:** 2026-08-03
**Issuer:** Human Steward (`fyremael`)
**Recognized Human Stewards:** `fyremael`, `jimsteeg`
**Scope:** Grand Intellect and Grand Challenge Labs governance staffing
**Duration:** Until superseded by a later Human Steward directive

## Direction

During the bootstrap stage, at least one Human Steward is the only required
human participation for an operation that exercises reserved human authority.
The recognized Human Stewards are `fyremael` and `jimsteeg`. Every other Minder,
Council, and Executor office may be staffed by agents. The absence of an
additional human contributor, reviewer, owner, or environment approver is not
an activation blocker while this directive is in force.

Each exact campaign, receipt, disposition, or other reserved-power operation
must identify one **acting Human Steward**. Either recognized Human Steward may
act. A second Human Steward signature is not required unless a governing
instrument expressly requires concurrence. The acting Steward is individually
accountable for the exact authorization and evidence named in that operation.

This is a temporary staffing policy. It does not abolish any office, waive an
office obligation, or transfer the Human Steward office's reserved powers.
Human onboarding remains intended, but it is not a prerequisite for current
operation.

## Required controls

Agent staffing and Human Steward action remain subject to the Constitution:

1. Every review identifies the agent, invocation or session, exact revision,
   office, discharged obligations, evidence, findings, and residual
   uncertainty.
2. A proposal author may not supply its sole verification or its Adversary or
   Referee disposition.
3. The same agent invocation or session may not act as both Adversary and
   Referee for one decision.
4. No agent may certify the full chain of its own proposal.
5. A distinct agent identity means a separately recorded invocation or session
   with an independent role brief and review artifact. Relabelling one response
   or replaying the same output does not create independence.
6. The Human Steward office retains the only human authority for constitutional
   promulgation, changes to office powers, destructive disposal,
   safety-critical deployment, public commitments, major irreversible resource
   allocation, and autonomous escalation of agent permissions.
7. Every reserved-power record must name exactly one acting Human Steward from
   the recognized roster and bind that person to the exact revision, packet, or
   disposition being authorized.
8. A person may act as independent reviewer or acting Human Steward for one
   exact operation, but may not be counted as both for that same operation.
9. Agents may prepare, test, route, and record proposals. They may not
   impersonate a Human Steward or manufacture a Steward's authenticated
   approval.

## GitHub operating effect

While this directive is effective:

- required-review controls use distinct agent findings plus authorization from
  one named acting Human Steward where a reserved power is involved;
- campaign and receipt records may maintain an allowlist of recognized Human
  Stewards, but each completed receipt records one accountable acting signer;
- independent-human approval, CODEOWNER approval, last-push approval, and
  human environment-review requirements remain deferred and nonblocking unless
  a governing instrument expressly activates them;
- strict required checks, resolved conversations, stale-review dismissal,
  protected branches, immutable releases, and zero bypass actors remain in
  force; and
- automation must report agent identity and evidence honestly. A green check,
  merge, reaction, or generated review does not by itself discharge an office
  obligation.

## Current constitutional proposal

For `GI-AMEND-0001`, the activation packet requires:

1. one non-author agent Adversary finding;
2. one different non-author agent Referee finding for the same exact packet;
3. approval by one acting Human Steward from the recognized roster; and
4. every other exact-commit, test, acceptance, and promulgation condition in
   the amendment.

The campaign must name its acting Human Steward before receipt completion. A
recognized Human Steward who supplied the independent non-author approval for a
particular admission PR may not also count as the acting Steward for that same
admission. Additional human Adversary or Referee signatures are not required
while this directive is effective.

## Appointment effect

`jimsteeg` is appointed to the Human Steward office effective upon protected
merge of the amendment carrying this text. The appointment is prospective. It
does not retroactively convert an earlier independent review, comment, reaction,
or merge disposition into Human Steward action. Earlier records retain the
authority and role attribution that applied when they were made unless a later
properly authenticated Steward record expressly ratifies a bounded prior act.

## Boundaries

This directive changes staffing, not substantive authority. It does not:

- make GitHub a constitutional or production semantic authority;
- alter AETHER's production semantic boundary;
- grant any agent or Council office mathematical certification authority;
- weaken MATHCERT admission requirements;
- convert candidate standards or proposals into accepted artifacts;
- authorize either Human Steward to count simultaneously as independent
  reviewer and acting Steward for one exact operation; or
- authorize an agent to merge or approve its own proposal where repository
  policy forbids that action.

## Review and supersession

A recognized Human Steward should review this directive at least once every 90
days and whenever a human is onboarded into or removed from a governance role.
A missed review does not silently terminate the directive; it creates a recorded
governance follow-up.

Only a later Human Steward directive may supersede or end this staffing mode or
change the recognized roster. The superseding record must state the effective
time, transition obligations, and which human-independence controls become
active.
