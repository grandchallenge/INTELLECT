# GI-STEWARD-0002: Minimum steady-state human authorization

**Status authority:** `governance/constitutional_authority_schedule.json`
**Issued:** 2026-08-09
**Issuer:** Human Steward (`fyremael`)
**Ordinary Human Steward:** `fyremael`
**Recovery owner:** `jimsteeg`
**Supersedes:** `GI-STEWARD-0001`, only when selected by the protected authority schedule
**Scope:** Grand Intellect and Grand Challenge Labs governance staffing
**Duration:** Until superseded by a later exact Human Steward directive

## Direction

Ordinary governed decisions require one substantive, authenticated,
role-bound authorization by the named Human Steward after inspection of one
exact-revision packet. That authorization is the sole ordinary human action
counted for the decision.

`fyremael` is the ordinary Human Steward. `jimsteeg` provides secure recovery
capacity and may serve as an optional bounded reviewer when separately named.
`jimsteeg` is not a mandatory reviewer or second signer for routine decisions.
Account setup, two-factor-authentication setup, recovery activity, and
mechanical merge clicks are reported separately and are not counted as the
substantive Steward authorization.

## Exact-packet control

Each governed decision must bind:

1. one exact subject revision and packet digest;
2. one non-author agent Adversary finding;
3. one different non-author agent Referee finding from a distinct session;
4. one authenticated authorization by the named acting Human Steward; and
5. the protected integration and resulting settings or record readback.

Any subject-head, packet-contract, staffing, receipt, or evidence-identity
change invalidates prior findings and Steward authorization. Automation may
assemble, validate, route, and replay the packet. It may not manufacture the
Steward authorization, approve, ratify, certify, activate, or exercise a
reserved human power.

## Recovery boundary

Recovery activity does not silently become an ordinary decision or
ordinary-review requirement. The protected schedule carries four distinct,
fail-closed protocols:

- **Steward replacement:** verified permanent incapacity or exhausted account
  recovery activates a standing, exact authorization granted by `fyremael` in
  this directive. `jimsteeg` may then act only as a single-use continuity
  authorizer for one protected roster transition. The trigger requires two
  independent records, including one external identity, platform-support, or
  legal record; the exact packet still requires distinct agent Adversary and
  Referee findings. The replacement must be an authenticated human other than
  `jimsteeg`. The recovery owner may neither appoint themself nor exercise any
  ordinary Steward power before or after that transition.
- **Account recovery:** verified account-access loss may trigger bounded
  restoration of the incumbent's existing access without a fresh incumbent
  authorization. Restoration may not change roles or permissions; it requires
  credential rotation, session revocation, and exact access readback. Any later
  role or permission change requires an authenticated ordinary-Steward exact
  packet. If all account-recovery routes are exhausted, the matter routes to
  the Steward-replacement protocol instead of assuming unavailable credentials.
- **Organization deletion:** only an explicit destructive intent by the
  ordinary Human Steward may trigger review. A fresh exact packet and
  contemporaneous Human Steward authorization are required; the recovery owner
  cannot authorize or execute deletion alone, and a verified export plus
  cooling-off period is mandatory.
- **Equivalent irreversible change:** a documented equivalence finding may
  route an operation to the organization-deletion safeguards. Ambiguity fails
  closed; it never permits unilateral recovery-owner action.

This directive does not pre-authorize any recovery or destructive operation.

## Activation, rollback, and grandfathering

This text is time-stable: its current effectiveness is determined only by the
protected authority schedule. Custody, review, a green check, or merge of this
file does not activate it.

The schedule may select this directive only through
`GI-HUMAN-GOVERNANCE-TRANSITION-001`, after organization-wide secure-only 2FA
is enabled with no noncompliant member, the exact review packet is complete,
and the ordinary Human Steward has supplied one packet-bound authorization.

Rollback requires a later exact Human Steward directive and may not silently
restore `GI-STEWARD-0001`. Decisions completed before the effective timestamp
retain their recorded staffing and authorization rules. In-flight decisions
must either finish under their already authorized exact packet or restart under
this directive; evidence and roles may not be relabelled retroactively.
