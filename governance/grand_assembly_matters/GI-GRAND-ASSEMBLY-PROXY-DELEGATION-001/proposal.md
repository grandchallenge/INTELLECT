# GI-GRAND-ASSEMBLY-PROXY-DELEGATION-001

**Title:** Fifteen-office terminal gate for proxy-delegation governance

**Status:** Before the Grand assembly

**Submitted:** 2026-08-12

**Passage rule:** full quorum and unanimous consent of five Minder and ten Council offices

## Clarification

The Constitution distinguishes five Minder offices from ten Council offices.
The earlier 10/10 Council records correctly satisfied their exact Council
motions and remain valid. Because the proxy-delegation proposal affects the
whole epistemic metabolism, its terminal policy decision requires the broader
fifteen non-operational offices.

Executor and Human Steward are operational offices. They do not vote in this
assembly. The Human Steward retains the later, separate authenticated authority
decision.

## Motion

Adopt prospectively for this proxy-delegation programme:

1. The committee terminal packet must contain differentiated findings from the
   Possibility, Reality, Purpose, Continuity, and Capacity Minders as well as
   all ten Council offices.
2. All fifteen reviews must bind the same frozen terminal-packet digest and use
   distinct reviewer and session identities.
3. Passage requires 15/15 identical affirmative office dispositions. Missing,
   stale, duplicate, dissenting, abstaining, or drifted records fail closed.
4. The five Minders must respectively preserve alternatives, demand
   disconfirming evidence, evaluate purpose and reversal, preserve lineage and
   scope, and govern suspension, supersession, abandonment, and disposal.
5. Earlier 10/10 records remain valid historical predecessors but cannot be
   counted as reviews of the future terminal packet.
6. A successful fifteen-office disposition submits the exact packet for a
   separate authenticated Human Steward decision. It creates no proxy authority.

The rule applies to this programme's terminal policy gate, not to each future
routine task. A later exact governance action may revise it prospectively while
preserving all prior records and reasons.
