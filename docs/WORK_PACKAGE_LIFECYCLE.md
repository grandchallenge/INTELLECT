# Work-Package Lifecycle

A work package is the atomic governed campaign of the Grand Intellect. It is larger than a task and smaller than an indefinite programme. It has a purpose, acceptance criteria, evidence, a decision, an inheritance record, and a deliberate active-set outcome.

## Charter

Question: Why does this work exist?

Required: purpose, scope, constraints, acceptance criteria, and stakeholders. The gate blocks vague ambition from entering the institutional pipeline.

## Generation

Question: What materially different possibilities exist?

Each alternative identifies a mechanism, assumptions, and a discriminating test. Parameter or wording changes alone are not material variation.

## Specification

Question: What exactly must be true?

The specification records claims, interfaces, evaluation contract, and reversal conditions.

## Realization

Question: What artifact embodies the candidate?

A realization identifies artifacts and executable verification commands. Deviations are recorded rather than silently absorbed.

## Confrontation

Question: What resisted the claim?

A valid Contact Record names a method capable of producing an adverse result and records uncertainty.

## Judgment

Question: Which candidate best serves the declared purpose?

Judgment records rationale, trade-offs, selected alternatives, and reversal conditions. Evidence constrains judgment; purpose completes it.

## Integration

Question: What must future agents inherit?

Memory preserves knowledge, reasons, scope, limitations, retrieval tags, terminology, dependencies, and artifact lineage.

## Disposal

Question: What should no longer occupy active cognitive space?

Completion requires an explicit active-set decision, recovery path or deletion authority, and residual frontier.

## Complete and reopening

Completion closes an institutional cycle; it does not exhaust the subject. New evidence, changed purpose, failed assumptions, changed dependencies, or renewed feasibility may reopen the package to Generation without rewriting the prior cycle.
