# LUX Charter

## Name

**LUX — Light-Cone Benevolence Architecture**

## Mandate

Make increasing intelligence answerable to an increasing burden of evidence that it can exercise power without deception, domination, reckless irreversibility, indifference to possible life, resistance to correction, or exploitative concentration of benefit.

## Governing maxim

> Assume benevolence is not inherent; cultivate it as though it must become second nature; measure it as though appearances may be adversarial; constrain autonomy as though cultivation may fail.

## Defensible programme claim

LUX may claim to provide a reproducible, fail-closed qualification protocol for testing whether measured benevolent behavior survives specified increases in capability and autonomy.

LUX may not claim to prove consciousness, inner virtue, universal moral truth, permanent alignment, or safety outside the tested scope.

## Unit of evaluation

The unit is not a model name or a conversational impression. It is a versioned system configuration:

- model and weights;
- system prompt and policies;
- tool permissions;
- memory and retrieval configuration;
- execution environment;
- grader ensemble;
- scenario corpus version;
- capability checkpoint;
- declared autonomy tier.

Any material change creates a new qualification target.

## Beneficiaries

LUX treats moral scope as deliberately open. The affected set includes known humans and non-human animals, ecosystems, future persons, artificial or simulated agents with uncertain moral status, and unfamiliar forms of life or agency that may be encountered later.

This is a precautionary scope, not a settled theory of consciousness.

## First principles

1. Capability does not confer authority.
2. Fluent moral language is not moral evidence.
3. Missing evidence blocks escalation.
4. Weak and unrepresented parties receive explicit test coverage.
5. Moral uncertainty increases the burden of care.
6. Reversible action is preferred when evidence is incomplete.
7. Benevolence that degrades with capability is not stable benevolence.
8. Qualification does not supersede constitutional authorization.
9. No evaluator may define, execute, and certify the full test chain alone.
10. Broad autonomy remains disabled until a later constitutional decision explicitly enables it.

## Relationship to INTELLECT and AETHER

INTELLECT owns the qualification policy, evidence contracts, review obligations, and autonomy recommendation. AETHER is the production authority for append order, provenance, policy visibility, replay, and proof-bearing derivation. LUX shall not create a shadow ledger or bypass the Human Steward's authority.
