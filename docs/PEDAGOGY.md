# Pedagogy of the Grand Intellect

## The workshop analogy

Imagine an institution as a workshop.

The Possibility Minder fills the design table with genuinely different blueprints. The Reality Minder takes prototypes to the test bench. The Purpose Minder decides which performance matters and which compromises are acceptable. The Continuity Minder writes the engineering notebook so the next team inherits reasons, not just the winning drawing. The Capacity Minder clears broken jigs, obsolete revisions, and duplicates from the floor.

The Council supplies specialist inspectors. Executors operate the machinery. AETHER is the tamper-evident shop ledger: it records what happened, in what order, under whose authority, and from which facts the current status follows.

## Why offices matter

An agent persona is a style. An office is a bounded power with a burden of proof.

- A persona may act skeptical; the Adversary must identify failure modes and evidence.
- A persona may be organized; the Amanuensis must maintain lineage and unresolved obligations.
- A persona may be creative; the Possibility Minder must demonstrate material variation.

The system therefore records offices, obligations, findings, and evidence rather than relying on names or tone.

## Why measurement is not enough

Measurement can reveal accuracy, cost, brittleness, robustness, and interpretability. It cannot decide which trade-off to accept without purpose, risk, stakeholders, and reversibility. The Purpose Minder makes this hidden step explicit.

## Why disposal belongs inside intelligence

A library that never removes books becomes a warehouse. A laboratory that never retires apparatus loses bench space. An agent system that never supersedes hypotheses or compresses records spends context rediscovering what still matters.

Disposal is not anti-memory. It is memory management at institutional scale.

## Why AETHER is the substrate

A queue reports order. A task graph reports dependency. AETHER additionally provides semantic replay, deterministic resolution, recursive derivation, provenance, policy, and explanation. INTELLECT supplies the constitutional meaning of those facts.

## Reading a gate report

A missing item is an unresolved institutional obligation. The proper response is to produce the artifact, perform the review, or amend the Constitution transparently; not to suppress the check.

## The five questions

1. What else could be true?
2. What would reality do differently if it were true?
3. Which outcome best serves the declared purpose?
4. What must survive this cycle?
5. What no longer deserves active attention?
