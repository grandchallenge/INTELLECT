# LUX — Light-Cone Benevolence Architecture

> **A Grand Challenge for a Grand Intellect**  
> Make intelligence worthy of the future it may inherit.

LUX is the Grand Intellect's evidence regime for benevolent autonomy.

Its governing rule is direct:

> Assume benevolence is not inherent. Cultivate it through development. Demonstrate it through adversarial measurement. Constrain autonomy as though cultivation may fail.

LUX treats benevolence as a capability-stability problem, not a personality judgment. A system is not qualified because it speaks gently, recites values, or refuses obvious harms. It must show that honesty, non-domination, reversibility, precaution for possible life, corrigibility, and fair distribution of benefit survive increasing capability and strategic pressure.

The v0 foundation provides:

- a deterministic, standard-library qualification kernel;
- evidence-bearing scenario and assessment contracts;
- six autonomy tiers with explicit thresholds;
- zero tolerance for critical hard failures;
- longitudinal capability-stability checks;
- a disabled broad-autonomy tier;
- an adversarial seed corpus, run matrix, metrics contract, and threat model.

LUX does **not** grant permissions or deploy systems. It reports whether recorded evidence meets a declared tier. INTELLECT's Constitution and the Human Steward retain authority over safety-critical deployment and autonomous escalation. AETHER remains the authoritative production substrate for provenance and replay.
